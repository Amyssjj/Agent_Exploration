<build>
1. Project digital-me-recipe: film/index.html plus film/film.js (ES module) plus film/preview.js. The stage is 1920x1080 with perspective 2000px (origin 960 540), and #world is preserve-3d. The camera is a matrix3d look-at inverse (translate z +2000) on #world. World units are px at 1080p at 2000 units from the lens. window.seek(t) sets the camera and every scene from t alone, then awaits all pending img.decode(). There are no CSS animations or timers, and no state carries between seeks. Moving positions (the ball, the hopped mark) are pure functions of t. window.FILM exports FPS, END, B and the fast windows, and window.__ready gates rendering.
2. Write the camera first, in camera(t): stations along the path (wordmark at z 0, focal screen at z 2300, Refactor at z 4800, lanes at y -900 z 7400, phone, bin, promise, dock, hands, Capture, Recall cylinder, Own rail, Distill, Compound, Carry, CTA, reel). Poses come from look(target, dist, yaw, pitch), mix, orbit and drift, all keyed on B().
3. Type: await document.fonts.load for every face before any layout. Line measures each glyph with a Range at 4x size (auth 4), places glyphs as absolute spans, and scales by 1/4. Label is a single-plane version for lines whose glyphs never separate. Icons are authored at 6–12x and scaled down.
4. Grounding and capture: Playwright (playwright-core, Chrome for Testing chromium-1217). tools/capture-site.mjs opens a 3840x2160 viewport with documentElement zoom 2, sets the theme through localStorage "motus-theme", clicks the demo tab, takes a CDP screencast, crops to the component's selector, and writes 24 fps H.264 to footage/site/<name>-<dark|light>.mp4.
5. tools/extract_seq.py: 24 fps JPEG sequences into film/assets/seq/ plus a manifest.json. Focal screens: claude and codex crops at 1600 wide, gblur 13; openclaw and hermes phone crops (645x1306 at 861,86), gblur 4.6, gain 0.8. The wall: 24 clips at 64 frames each, 720 wide, gblur 3.6, gain 0.75 dark / 0.5 light. The hands: hands-a, 216 alpha WebP frames through hqdn3d=2.5:2.5:5:5, deband, colorlevels black-in 0.035, format=rgba, lumakey threshold 0 tolerance 0.03 softness 0.10, q 86. In bar 7 only the right (human) half is used, via clip-path inset(0 0 0 50%). In bar 10 the full plate is used at rx -14. Wall screens also get a runtime filter: brightness, plus blur up to 14px within 1500 units of the lens.
6. Crumple, pre-rendered offline: tools/crumple.html (WebGL2 on a 1600x1600 canvas, a 160x40 grid textured with grey "Re-brief it." on #141415, ridged-noise creases squeezed onto a noisy sphere, flat-shaded) and tools/crumple.mjs, which writes 14 alpha WebP frames (crumple over about 10, then the ball turns).
7. Audio: tools/analyze_song.py (numpy STFT, bands sub 25–120, mids 300–3000 and hats 6k–11k Hz) and tools/song_map.py (sub-band kick peaks, a least-squares straight grid fitted to the on-beat kicks, an 8th-note band-energy map, full stops) write audio/omw-map.json. tools/foley.py synthesizes three hits, placed by their measured peak at 0.32 gain: a touch at 7·4·3, a crumple at 8·2·1 + 0.05 s and a bin hit at 8·3·1. It pads the song to END at 48 kHz, then runs two-pass loudnorm I=-14 TP=-1 LRA=11 linear and writes audio/mix.wav (24-bit). The preview uses film/assets/audio/mix.m4a.
8. tools/render.mjs serves film/ over local http. Modes: stills, sheet (the pickup plus every 8th note at +0.03 s, tiled as two 8x8 sheets), draft (960x540, one sample per frame, with audio) and final. The final pass runs at viewport 1920x1080, deviceScaleFactor 2, --disable-lcd-text and --force-color-profile=srgb, capturing with page.screenshot({scale:'device'}) to get true 3840x2160. Motion blur is a 180° shutter over 1/48 s centered on t: 5 samples normally and 15 inside the FAST windows (2 frames before to 1 frame after the whips and fly-throughs). Each frame always pipes 15 images (the 5 samples sent 3 times each) into ffmpeg: tmix=frames=15 with equal weights, select every 15th, setpts=N/24/TB, prores_ks profile 3, yuv422p10le, written to renders/final-video.mov. The run took about 11 minutes.
9. Deliver: mux mix.wav into deliver/digital-me-film-4k-master.mov (-c:v copy, pcm_s24le, -shortest). The 4K MP4 is libx264 -preset slow -crf 14, high@5.2, yuv420p, faststart, AAC 320k. A 1080p copy is lanczos, crf 16. Check the final at -14.03 LUFS, 3840x2160, 24 fps, 36.667 s.
10. Preview: .claude/launch.json runs python http.server 8765 on film/. index.html?preview adds a scrubber with bar ticks, audio, Space to play, the arrow keys to step a frame, and Shift+arrow to step a beat.
</build>

<gotchas>
Load every font before the first measurement. When layout ran before Inter loaded, narrow letters got extra space and lines sat off-center.
Chrome's CDP Page.captureScreenshot ignores deviceScaleFactor and returned 1920x1080. Use page.screenshot({scale:'device'}) for the 4K pass.
5 subframes still strobed on whips, fly-throughs and the reel. Use 15 subframes in the fast windows.
The site's fit-to-container script (PdmMockFit) reads getBoundingClientRect, which doubles under root zoom, so the lifecycle demos came out mis-cropped. Pin its scale with injected CSS (.pdm-agents .pdm-mock-canvas{transform:scale(1.21)}).
"flat" is a reserved word in GLSL ES 3.0, and it broke the crumple shader.
The hero hands clip is a low-bitrate web encode and bands at 4K. Denoise and deband it before the luma key.
UI text stayed readable at blur 3–4. It needs sigma 13 on the focal screens, plus runtime depth blur near the lens.
The laptop-to-phone hop first crossed the lens. Ease the camera back mid-hop.
Carried state made scrubbing wrong. Every position must be computed from t.
The render server's favicon 404 is harmless.
A wait loop using pgrep can match its own command line and never exit.
Never set opacity or filter on a preserve-3d element; fade its wrapper instead.
Keep type planes authored large and scaled down, with no will-change.
Use only the site's own agent SVGs, never LobeHub redraws. A downloaded openai-color.svg turned out to be a saved 404 page.
The song is licensed through Artlist to the user and must not be redistributed. All fonts are OFL.
</gotchas>
